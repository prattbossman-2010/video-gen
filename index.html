    How it works

    1. Type your script. Blank lines split scenes.
    2. Press Generate Video. The AI searches open-license sources (Openverse, Wikimedia, plus Pexels if you add a free key), picks the best clip per scene, trims everything to the voiceover length and assembles the final cut.
    3. Preview, then hit Download Video. No watermark, no upload, nothing leaves your device except media searches.

    Browsing the whole web (recommended)

    For security, browsers block pages from reading other sites directly. To unlock the full power - HD videos from Pexels, Pixabay and Mixkit plus reliable embedded voices - double-click Start Studio.bat instead of opening index.html directly. It starts a tiny private helper on your machine (nothing is uploaded anywhere) and opens the app. The app detects it automatically.

    AI brain (recommended - paste your keys)

    Keys power the AI director (scene planning, perfect visual matching) and premium voices. They are stored only in this browser. You can also put them in js/config.js or the .env file.

    Voiceovers & audio

    Voices come from free online speech services. If your network blocks them, preview reads scenes aloud with your device's built-in reader. For audio inside the exported file you have three options - none need any setup:

    • Background music: add any music file in step 1 - it is always embedded in the export.
    • Screen-share capture (Chrome/Edge): when exporting, tick the screen-share audio option and choose "This tab" with audio sharing on - it records everything you hear, including the built-in voices.
    • Different network: voice services sometimes work from other connections.

    Tips

    • Keep this tab visible while downloading the final video.
    • If a scene used a blocked source you'll see a warning before export.
    • Write concrete visual nouns in your script or style description (e.g. "ocean waves at sunset") - the AI matches footage by those words.
